Natural order
Everything begins with honouring nature: the sun and the moon, the stars, the rivers, the trees. The body is a small universe; what lives outside us lives inside us too.

Earth at the bottom, the ground that carries everything. On earth comes water. Where there is water, fire can burn; where there is fire, air rises. Above them all, open space, and the sound of Om.

This is how the elements sit in nature, in the body, and in the five-ring pagoda of Japan, one element to each floor in the same order. Every element lives in every other, yet this is their natural order: simple enough for a child, deep enough for a lifetime of looking.

The way of the sounds

Everything starts with honouring nature: the sun and the moon, the stars, the rivers, the trees and the birds. The body is a yantra, a small universe; the sun, the moon and the river live in us too.

Everything begins as vision. In the silence of Om, pure violet, a creative seeing is born.

Vision flows into Water, U. Water has no colour of its own; it reflects. It is creativity, the creative energy of a human being, the mood within us. Yet water needs ground: without earth it has nowhere to land and cannot move.

Earth, A, is the one true colour: grounding gold. Living matter, the body, our daily work, the joy of being here in a body.

On that ground Fire, I, is kindled. Not a colour but pure energy: the strength of the body, rising to do the work the vision asks for.

The work comes home in Air, O. No colour, only connection. Beyond sight we feel one another; the fruit of all work is to serve each other's growth.

Then the throat, A-U-I-O, gives it a voice in every colour of the rainbow. Yoga names four levels of speech: Vaikhari, the spoken word everybody hears; Madhyama, the inner word of thought and feeling; Pashyanti, the seeing word, an image before words; Para, the silent source. A truth spoken on all four is simple enough for a child of four and deep enough to satisfy a philosopher.

Sing each vowel on one breath, plainly, and let the next breath come by itself.
